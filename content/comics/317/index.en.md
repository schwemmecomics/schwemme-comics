In this case, to cope with the financial burden of a €2 deposit.

Over the course of the Bergfest, some people develop an emotional attachment to their cups. No wonder, when you spend almost a whole week together.

It’s a perfectly valid, if not particularly welcome, strategy to have a beer poured into your cup to take away, so you can carry on drinking straight away the next day.
