On Black Monday we grieve a lost day of Bergfest, because the official begin was set to Tuesday a few years ago. As a result, the party was moved to Schwemme. If we can't party at the faculty, then we'll party five minutes away from it.

All guests should be wearing black that day, it is, after all, a funeral. Some Schwemmlings are so affected by this, they have to take a rest in storage.
