The time has come. Sort of an anniversary.

People have pointed out to me several times that some things in the comics are no longer up to date. After 365 comics, maybe it’s the right time to set a few things straight. Better late than never, or something like that?

I’ve been drawing comics for about two years now, the website has been up since the beginning of the year, and I’m excited to see how this project will continue to develop. None of this would be possible without Schwemme, the people who visit there, and you, the readers of these comics. Thank you all so much.
