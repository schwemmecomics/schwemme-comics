Beer consumption at the Bergfest usually runs into the high three figures. 

Despite having stocked up generously, the beer ran out on Bergfest Friday. Fortunately, the Schwemme isn’t far away, and the remaining supplies held out until a team (led by Frank) was able to bring in more.

Frank’s cap once again reveals hidden features.
